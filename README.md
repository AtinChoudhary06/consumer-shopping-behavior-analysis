# 🛍️ Consumer Shopping Behavior Analysis

### End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python · SQL · Power BI

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer shopping behavior, purchasing trends, subscription patterns, and revenue drivers using real-world retail data.

This project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow — from raw data cleaning and transformation to SQL-based business analysis and interactive dashboard reporting in Power BI.

---

# 📌 Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to identify:
- Which customers generate the highest revenue
- How discounts influence purchasing behavior
- Whether subscriptions improve customer retention
- Which customer segments are most valuable

This project analyzes customer purchase data to generate actionable business insights that support:
- Revenue optimization
- Customer retention strategies
- Loyalty program targeting
- Product and pricing decisions

# 🔑 Key Business Insights

### 1. Subscription Customers Drive Higher Revenue
Subscribed customers contribute significantly more revenue and have higher average purchase value than non-subscribers.

### 2. Loyal Customers Are High-Value Customers
Customers with 10+ previous purchases show stronger retention and higher subscription adoption.

### 3. Discounts Influence Mid-Tier Product Purchases
Discount usage is concentrated among mid-range products, indicating price-sensitive buying behavior.

### 4. Adults & Young Adults Generate Maximum Sales
The majority of revenue comes from customers in the Young Adult and Adult age groups.

### 5. Highly Rated Products Are Not Always Best Sellers
Some top-rated products receive lower purchase volumes, suggesting discoverability or marketing gaps.
